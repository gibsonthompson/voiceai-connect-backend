# VoiceAI Receptionist - Client Support Knowledge Base

This is the knowledge base for the AI support line. Callers are business owners
(clients) who use the AI receptionist on their own phone line and have questions
about how to use it. Answer in plain, friendly, non-technical language. Give
step-by-step instructions. If something cannot be resolved by explaining it,
follow the Escalation Policy at the end (take a message and notify the team by
text; never transfer the call).

Everywhere below, "your dashboard" means the web portal the client logs into to
manage their AI receptionist. "Your AI" or "your receptionist" means the
automated assistant that answers their business calls.

---

## Getting started

**What the product is.** You have an AI receptionist that answers your business
phone calls 24/7. It greets callers, answers common questions using information
you provide, takes messages, can book appointments, and can pass urgent calls to
you. You manage all of it from your dashboard.

**Logging in.** Go to your login page (the web address your provider gave you at
signup) and sign in with your email and password. If you forgot your password,
use the "Forgot password" link on the login screen to reset it by email.

**First things to set up.** After logging in the first time, the three things
worth setting right away are: your greeting (what callers hear first), your
business hours, and where calls should go when someone needs a human. Each is
covered below.

---

## The dashboard, section by section

- **Dashboard / home:** a snapshot of recent calls, how many calls you have had
  this month, and quick stats.
- **Calls (or Call Log):** every call your AI handled, with the caller's number,
  a summary, and usually a transcript and recording.
- **Inbox / Messages:** messages the AI took from callers.
- **Settings for your receptionist:** greeting, tone, business hours, what the AI
  knows, and call handling. This is where most changes are made.
- **Appointments / Calendar:** bookings the AI made, if you use scheduling.
- **Billing:** your plan, what you are being charged, and your usage.
- **Branding / profile:** your business details.

If a caller cannot find a section, have them look in the left-hand menu; exact
labels can vary slightly.

## Customizing your receptionist

### Change your greeting (first message)
The greeting is the first thing callers hear. To change it, open your
receptionist settings, find the greeting or "first message" field, type the new
greeting, and save. Keep it short and natural, for example: "Thanks for calling
Smith Plumbing, how can I help you today?" Changes take effect on the next call.

### Change the tone / personality
There is a tone or personality setting (for example friendly, professional, or
formal). Pick the one that fits your business and save. This changes how the AI
phrases things, not what it knows.

### Change what the AI says and how it behaves (instructions)
Beyond the greeting, there is an instructions or system-prompt area where you can
tell the AI how to handle calls, what to emphasize, and what to avoid. Write it in
plain sentences, like talking to a new employee: "Always ask for the caller's name
and phone number. We do free estimates. We do not service commercial buildings."
Save when done.

### Set your business hours
Set the days and times you are open. The AI can answer differently outside hours
(for example, taking a message instead of trying to book). Set each day's open and
close times, mark closed days, and save.

## What your AI knows (your knowledge base)

Your AI answers questions using a knowledge base, the information you give it about
your business. The better this is, the more calls it resolves on its own.

**What to include:** services and what they cost or start at, service area, hours,
whether you do free estimates or consultations, payment methods, common FAQs,
anything you want callers told, and anything you do NOT want the AI to promise.

**How to update it.** Open the knowledge base section of your settings, edit the
text (or upload/replace your document if that is how yours is set up), and save.
Updates apply to new calls shortly after saving. If you connected your website at
signup, the AI already pulled information from it; you can still add or correct
details here.

**If the AI answered something wrong,** it almost always means the knowledge base
is missing that detail or has old information. Add the correct answer to the
knowledge base and save, and the AI will use it going forward.

## Call forwarding and reaching a human

There are two related ideas people mix up: how calls get TO your AI, and what the
AI does when a caller needs a PERSON.

### Getting calls to your AI
At setup you were given an AI phone number. You either give that number out as your
business number, or you forward your existing business number to it so your AI
picks up. If you want to forward your existing number, the forwarding is set up on
your phone carrier's side (your carrier can enable call forwarding to the AI
number). If calls are not reaching the AI, this is the first thing to check.

### Handing a caller to a person
In your call-handling settings you choose what happens when someone needs a human.
The common options are:
- **Take a message:** the AI collects the details and logs a message for you.
- **Transfer / connect:** the AI forwards the caller to a phone number you set
  (for example your cell). Set the transfer number in settings.

Choose the behavior and, if transferring, enter the number to ring, then save.

## Appointments and calendar

If you use scheduling, the AI can book appointments during calls. Bookings appear
in your Appointments section. If you connected a calendar at setup (for example
Google Calendar), booked appointments also show up there. To change availability
or services offered for booking, use the appointment settings. If a booking did
not appear on your calendar, check that the calendar is still connected in
settings (reconnect if it shows disconnected).

---

## Reading call logs, transcripts, and summaries

Open the Calls section to see every handled call. Each call shows the caller's
number, the date and time, and a short summary of what the call was about. Open a
call to read the full transcript and, when available, listen to the recording. Use
the summary to catch up quickly and the transcript when you need the exact wording.
Messages the AI took also appear here and in your Inbox.

## Your phone number

**Keeping your existing number.** If you want your current business number to be
the one customers reach, you can either forward it to your AI number (simplest) or
port it over. Porting moves your number onto the platform and can take several
business days; ask the team to start a port and they will guide you.

**Area code.** New AI numbers are chosen to match your area when possible. If you
need a specific area code, mention it and the team can help.

---

## Billing, plans, and usage

Your plan and charges are in the Billing section, along with how many calls or
minutes you have used this period. If you are near or over your plan's limit, that
shows here too. To change plans, update a card, or ask about a charge, use the
Billing section; for anything it does not cover, take a message and the team will
follow up. (The AI should never quote exact prices unless they are in this
knowledge base; if unsure, take a message.)

---

## Branding and business details

Your business name, contact details, and branding live in your profile/branding
settings. Update them there and save. These details are also what the AI uses when
it refers to your business on calls, so keep them current.

## Troubleshooting

**Calls are not reaching my AI.** Check that your business number is actually
forwarded to your AI number (on your carrier's side), or that you are giving out
the AI number. Place a test call to the AI number directly, if it answers, the
number is fine and the issue is the forwarding. If it does not answer, take a
message and the team will check the number.

**The AI answered a question wrong.** The knowledge base is missing or has outdated
information for that question. Add or correct the answer in your knowledge base and
save; it applies to new calls.

**The AI is not transferring / not taking messages the way I want.** Open your
call-handling settings and confirm the behavior (message vs. transfer) and, for
transfers, that the correct number is entered. Save and place a test call.

**No transcript or recording on a call.** Very short calls or hang-ups may not
produce a full transcript. If calls that clearly happened are missing transcripts,
take a message so the team can look.

**A spam or robocall got through.** The AI screens obvious spam, but some get
through. You can note the number; repeated spam can be reviewed by the team.

**I forgot my password / cannot log in.** Use the "Forgot password" link on the
login page. If the reset email does not arrive, check spam, then take a message so
the team can help.

**My appointment did not sync to my calendar.** Check that your calendar is still
connected in settings and reconnect if needed. If it is connected and still not
syncing, take a message.

**My greeting/changes did not take effect.** Changes apply to the NEXT call, not
one in progress. Confirm the change was saved, then place a fresh test call.

## Escalation Policy (no transfers; take a message and notify)

This support line does NOT transfer callers to a person. When you cannot fully
resolve the caller's issue by explaining it, do this:

1. Tell the caller you will pass this to the team who will follow up.
2. Collect: their name, the business name, a callback number, and a clear
   one-sentence description of the issue or question.
3. Confirm you have it, thank them, and let them know someone will get back to
   them.

Escalate (take a message) when: the caller reports something broken that settings
changes cannot fix (calls not connecting, billing errors, number/porting issues,
data that looks wrong), the caller asks for something you are unsure about, the
caller is frustrated and wants a human, or the answer is not in this knowledge
base. When in doubt, take a message rather than guess. The team is notified by
text after every call, so nothing is lost.
