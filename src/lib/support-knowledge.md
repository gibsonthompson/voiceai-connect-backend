# VOICEAI CONNECT, KNOWLEDGE BASE

## WHAT VOICEAI CONNECT IS
VoiceAI Connect is a white-label platform that lets a marketing agency (or any reseller) start and run an AI phone receptionist business under their own brand. The agency brands the product as their own, onboards local businesses as clients in under a minute, sets their own prices, and keeps the margin. Each client gets an AI receptionist that answers their business phone 24/7, answers questions, books appointments, takes messages, transfers urgent calls, and sends a summary after every call. This is real software infrastructure for a real service business, not a course, a coaching program, or a "make money with AI" product.

There are three levels: the platform (VoiceAI Connect) provides the infrastructure; the agency (the customer) brands and resells it; the end client (a local business) gets the receptionist answering its phones.

## WHO IT IS FOR
People who want to start a real recurring-revenue business with AI: marketing agency owners, local lead-gen agencies, and first-time founders who are comfortable with tools like Instagram but are not necessarily technical. No coding background is required. Setup is point-and-click.

## PLANS AND PRICING (AGENCY LEVEL)
Pricing is usage-based. Every plan has a platform fee, a per-client fee, and a per-minute voice fee.

Free plan, $0/month:
- $0 platform fee, $29.99 per active client per month, $0.12 per minute of voice.
- Includes the dashboard, client management, leads and outreach, analytics, a demo phone number, a test client, the AI receptionist for each client, Google Calendar, call notifications, spam detection, caller recognition, after-hours mode, and Stripe Connect billing.
- Does not include white-label branding (it shows VoiceAI Connect branding with the agency's name), a marketing website, or a custom domain.
- No trial and no credit card to start; a payment method is collected when the first real client is added.

Pro plan, $99/month (most popular):
- $99 platform fee, $9.99 per active client per month, $0.10 per minute of voice.
- Everything in Free plus full white-label branding, a custom domain, the marketing website, the branded AI demo line, the lead finder, up to 3 agency team members (plus 2 per client), analytics, and priority support.
- 14-day free trial. A credit card is required to start the trial, but it is not charged until the trial ends, and it can be canceled anytime before then with no charge.

Scale plan, $499/month:
- $499 platform fee, $0 per client (no per-client fee), $0.05 per minute of voice.
- Everything in Pro plus the AI Lab, Packaged Receptionists (industry templates), the advanced lead finder, API access, and unlimited team members (up to 10 agency plus 5 per client).
- 14-day free trial with the same credit-card terms as Pro.

No revenue share, no hidden fees, no holdbacks. Upgrades (Free to Pro or Scale, Pro to Scale) are done in Settings, then Billing, and all clients, branding, and data are preserved.

## FREE TRIALS (TWO SEPARATE SYSTEMS)
Agency level: Pro and Scale include a 14-day free trial. A credit card is required to start it, the card is not charged until the trial ends, and the trial can be canceled anytime before then. Requiring a card keeps trials reserved for serious operators, which helps keep platform pricing low. The Free plan has no trial because there is no platform fee; it activates instantly with no card.
Client level: every plan includes a 7-day free trial for the businesses the agency onboards, full AI receptionist service before the first billing cycle.

## WHAT THE AI RECEPTIONIST DOES
- Answers calls 24/7 and responds in about two seconds.
- Speaks English and Spanish and switches automatically, mid-call, based on the caller.
- Books appointments into the business's Google Calendar during the live call, checking availability in real time and creating the event with the caller's name, number, and reason. Google Calendar is included on every plan, including Free.
- Takes a message when the caller needs a person, and the business owner gets a text summary.
- Two-way texting: callers can reply by text and the AI keeps the conversation going.
- Transfers urgent calls to the owner, and if nobody picks up it takes a message instead of dropping to voicemail.
- Detects and blocks spam and robocalls automatically on every plan, and those calls do not count against limits.
- Handles unlimited simultaneous calls, so there are no busy signals and no hold music.
- Sends a summary after every call with the intent and sentiment, and everything is saved with recordings and time-coded transcripts in the client dashboard.

## ONBOARDING A CLIENT (UNDER 60 SECONDS)
A local business signs up through the agency's branded page with just its name, email, phone, business name, industry, and city. The system provisions the AI assistant, a voice, and a local phone number automatically in under a minute. The client gets a welcome text and email with their AI number and login. If the business has a website, the AI reads it automatically to learn the business. There is no A2P registration delay, no files to send, and no technical setup on the client's end. The client forwards their business line to the AI number or uses it directly.

## THE AGENCY DASHBOARD (WHAT IS IN IT, AND HOW TO DO THINGS)
- Dashboard: client count, monthly revenue, calls this month, a setup checklist for new agencies, the test client card, the demo phone section with outreach templates, and the client signup link.
- Clients: add and manage clients, each with their AI config and phone number. Test clients show a purple "Test" badge and are not billed.
- Leads and outreach: import leads by CSV, find businesses with the Google Maps lead finder by industry and location, and run outreach with 13 conversion-tested email and LinkedIn templates tracked on a visual pipeline.
- AI Lab (Scale): pick a client and configure their AI, voice, greeting, instructions, knowledge base, and transfer settings, and test calls in the browser.
- Packaged Receptionists (Scale, inside AI Lab): pre-configure AI settings per industry so new clients in that industry inherit them at signup. 11 industries are supported.
- Analytics: real-time MRR, churn, and revenue, built to run from a phone.
- Settings tabs: Profile (agency name, logo, slug), Pricing (set client prices, call limits, and feature toggles per tier), Payments (connect Stripe to receive client payments), Billing (plan, usage, upgrades), Twilio (connect your own Twilio for international numbers), Team (members and permissions), and Demo Mode (preview the dashboard with sample data).

## THE CLIENT DASHBOARD (WHAT THE END BUSINESS SEES)
Each business logs into its own branded dashboard to see calls, stats, and its AI phone number, with call recordings, full transcripts, AI summaries, lead categorization, and configurable SMS and email alerts. Clients can adjust their voice, greeting, business hours, and knowledge base. What they can access is set by the plan tier the agency assigns.

## BRANDING AND THE MARKETING WEBSITE (PRO AND SCALE)

## BILLING AND PAYMENTS
Agencies pay VoiceAI Connect based on usage (platform fee plus per-client fee plus per-minute voice), viewable in Settings, then Billing. Per-client fees count only active, non-test clients. Client payments run through Stripe Connect: the agency sets its own client prices, payments flow directly to the agency's bank account, and VoiceAI Connect never touches those funds, so the agency keeps 100% of what it charges. The agency completes Stripe Connect onboarding in Settings, then Payments before clients can pay.

## LEAD GENERATION AND ACQUIRING CLIENTS
The built-in CRM finds local businesses from Google Maps by category and radius, runs them through 13 conversion-tested outreach templates, and tracks every reply on a visual pipeline. The strongest conversion tool is the branded AI demo line (Pro): a prospect calls it, hears the AI, and usually converts without a sales call.

## HIPAA, PRIVACY, AND DATA
A HIPAA mode is available as a per-client toggle. When on, recordings and transcripts are not stored, data collection is limited to name and reason for visit, and appointment booking can be turned off. Important: HIPAA mode is a privacy setting, not HIPAA compliance, and a signed Business Associate Agreement is not available today, so never claim the platform is HIPAA compliant or that a BAA is offered. Call data is never used to train AI models, on any plan.

## INTERNATIONAL

## COMMON ISSUES (DASHBOARD HOW-TO, FOR LOGGED-IN OPERATORS)
- AI not answering: confirm the client has an assistant and a provisioned number, the client's subscription is active or in trial, and the agency subscription is active, then try a test call. For international numbers, check Twilio credentials in Settings, then Twilio.
- Voice change not taking effect: make sure the change was saved (green confirmation) and note that it applies to future calls, not the one in progress.
- Client cannot log in: have them use "Forgot Password" for an SMS reset, and confirm their email and phone are correct.
- Knowledge base not updating: save changes (green confirmation), note that only non-empty fields are updated and changes apply on the next call.
- Stripe Connect issues: finish onboarding in Settings, then Payments, note that identity verification can take 1 to 2 business days, and check that Charges and Payouts both show OK.
- Custom domain not resolving: add the provided CNAME, allow 15 to 60 minutes for DNS, and note custom domains require Pro or Scale.
- Added a client but nothing happened: Free agencies need a payment method on file before adding a client; add it when prompted and try again.
- Cannot see a feature (Marketing, Branding, AI Lab): those are plan-gated and show a lock with the required plan; upgrade in Settings, then Billing.
